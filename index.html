About the box

Fit for the life you came to Wānaka for

CrossFit Wanaka is home to everyday people who train for the simple love of moving their bodies and working hard. We believe CrossFit can lift anyone's fitness, so you can get more out of the mountains, the lake and everything else this place throws at you.

Based at The Gym Wanaka, we run coached classes morning and evening, six days a week. You won't just get fitter here, you'll become part of the crew.

  • Locally owned and coached, right here in Wānaka
  • Official CrossFit affiliate with certified coaches
  • Every workout scaled to your level, every single day
