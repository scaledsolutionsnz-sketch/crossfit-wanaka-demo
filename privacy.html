1. Overview

CrossFit Wanaka respects your privacy. This policy describes how personal information is handled when you use this website or contact us. This is placeholder content for the demo website and should be replaced with the business's final policy before launch.

2. What we collect

This website does not contain forms and does not collect personal information directly. If you contact us by phone or email, we hold the details you provide (such as your name, phone number and email address) so we can respond and manage your bookings or membership.

3. How we use your information

Contact details are used to reply to enquiries, manage class bookings and memberships, and share relevant updates about the gym. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

This site embeds services such as Google Maps and links to social media platforms. Those services operate under their own privacy policies when you interact with them.

5. Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, you may request access to or correction of the personal information we hold about you. Contact us on 021 469 496 to do so.
